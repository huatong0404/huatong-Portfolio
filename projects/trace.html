Trace

01 / A publication as a map

A Newspaper

Nearly forgotten today, The Allegheny Feminist carried news, arguments, events, and addresses between feminists in Pittsburgh. It began in 1976 and later continued as Network News.

Those issues became the main source for Trace’s research and imagery. I translated their record of everyday life, political pressure, and collective hope into a game of movement and chance. I think of it as documentation you can enter.

Publication history: University of Pittsburgh Archives & Special Collections ↗ · Pitt Rare Books ↗
