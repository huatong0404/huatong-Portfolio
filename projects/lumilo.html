Lumilo

Lumilo is a smart glasses project that helps teachers notice which students may need support during class. On Lumilo 2, I explored how recommendations could reflect classroom context while leaving room for teacher judgment. I created interview storyboards, revisited earlier research data, and prototyped the recommendation interface.
